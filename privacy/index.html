Privacy Policy

This policy explains how KWIZ.LIVE collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use our website, game, and related services.

Contact: For privacy questions or requests, email info@kwiz.live.

Who We Are

KWIZ.LIVE ("KWIZ", "we", "us", or "our") provides a multiplayer trivia game with live duels, solo play, topic generation, social feed, chat, clans, leaderboards, notifications, and related features.

Information We Collect

  • Launch waitlist: if you leave your email on kwiz.live to hear when KWIZ is available, we keep that address and the date you signed up. We use it only to tell you about the launch, we never share it, and you can ask us to remove it any time at info@kwiz.live.
  • Account information: username, email address, authentication provider identifiers, avatar, profile details, the birth month and year you give on the age screen, and sign-in records.
  • Google sign-in information: when you choose Google sign-in, we receive the Google account information needed to create and secure your KWIZ account, such as your name, email address, profile image, and Google user ID. We do not ask for access to Gmail, Drive, Contacts, Calendar, or other Google content.
  • Gameplay information: topics played, answers, scores, XP, ranks, streaks, missions, crowns, leaderboard position, duels, live event participation, and match history.
  • Community content: posts, comments, reactions, chat messages, clan activity, reports, blocks, usernames, profile images, custom topic names, and any images, audio, or attachments you choose to upload.
  • Location and nearby features: if you allow location, the app sends your device's position to our server, which turns it into a suburb, city, region and country for nearby players, clans and regional leaderboards. We keep only those place names, never the coordinates. Outside the cities we map ourselves, the coordinates are sent once to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service to look up the city name. You can hide your location or turn off nearby challenges in the app.
  • Device and technical information: IP address, device type, app version, operating system, server logs, security events, and push notification tokens. The app has no analytics or crash-reporting SDK of its own.
  • Purchases: when you buy coins or KWIZ+ through Google Play, we keep the purchase token, order ID, product and its state so we can grant it and handle refunds. Google processes the payment; we never see your card details.
  • Ads: the app can offer optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob, shown only when you choose to watch one. The AdMob SDK collects your device's advertising ID, IP address, ad interactions and diagnostic information, and Google uses them for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.
  • Website visits: we count visits to kwiz.live with self-hosted, cookie-free analytics (Umami): pages viewed, referrer, browser, device type and country. It sets no cookies, stores no device identifier, and builds no profile of you.
  • Support and moderation information: messages you send us, reports you submit, reported content, block lists, moderation decisions, and abuse-prevention records.

How We Use Information

  • Provide accounts, authentication, matchmaking, gameplay, leaderboards, chat, feed, clans, notifications, and support.
  • Generate and moderate quiz topics and questions.
  • Personalize your profile, XP, ranks, recommendations, and game state.
  • Detect spam, abuse, cheating, security threats, and policy violations.
  • Respond to reports, enforce our Terms and Community Guidelines, and protect users.
  • Analyze performance, fix bugs, maintain infrastructure, and improve KWIZ.
  • Comply with legal obligations and requests.

Google User Data

We use Google sign-in data only to authenticate you, create or connect your KWIZ account, display basic profile information you choose to use in KWIZ, secure your account, and provide support. We do not sell Google user data. We do not use Google user data for advertising. We do not transfer Google user data except as necessary to provide and secure KWIZ, comply with law, or with your direction.

How We Share Information

  • With other users: your username, avatar, public profile, gameplay results, leaderboard/rank information, posts, comments, reactions, clan activity, and content you choose to share may be visible to other users.
  • With service providers: we use vendors for hosting (Hetzner), file storage (Cloudflare R2), sign-in (Google), payments (Google Play), push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and place names (OpenStreetMap Nominatim). They process information for us under appropriate restrictions.
  • With Google for ads: if you watch a rewarded ad, the data described under "Ads" above goes to Google AdMob.
  • With our AI providers: when you create a custom topic, its name (and the category it sits in) is sent to AI model providers to write and check the quiz questions. When you set a profile picture, the photo you choose (or your Google profile photo, the first time you sign in with Google) is sent to an AI image provider to redraw it as a cartoon; we keep only the cartoon, not the photo. We do not send your name, email, or account details with either.
  • For safety and legal reasons: we may share information when needed to investigate abuse, protect users, enforce policies, comply with law, or respond to lawful requests.
  • Business changes: if KWIZ is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or asset transfer, information may be transferred subject to this policy or a successor policy.

Data Retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide KWIZ, maintain game integrity, resolve disputes, enforce our policies, comply with legal obligations, and operate safety systems. Some operational logs and abuse-prevention records may be retained for a limited period after account deletion where necessary for security, legal compliance, or fraud prevention.

Your Choices

  • You can update profile information in the app.
  • You can turn off push notifications through your browser, device, or app settings.
  • You can disable location sharing or nearby visibility in the app where available.
  • You can block users and report content or accounts in the app.
  • You can delete your account using the in-app flow described on our Account Deletion page.

Children

KWIZ is for people aged 16 and over. The app asks for your age when you sign up and closes accounts for anyone younger. If you believe someone under 16 has provided personal information to KWIZ, contact us at info@kwiz.live so we can review and take appropriate action.

Security

We use technical and organizational measures designed to protect information, including authentication controls, access limits, encrypted transport, monitoring, and moderation tools. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

International Use

KWIZ may be operated from and processed in countries other than where you live. By using KWIZ, you understand that information may be transferred to and processed in those locations.

Changes

We may update this policy as KWIZ changes. We will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice.
