Community Guidelines

KWIZ is competitive, social, and fast. Keep the rivalry fun, fair, and safe.

Be Respectful

Do not harass, threaten, bully, stalk, shame, dox, or target other users. Competitive banter is fine; abuse is not.

No Hateful Or Objectionable Content

Do not post, send, upload, generate, or promote content that attacks people based on protected characteristics, encourages violence, contains sexual exploitation, glorifies self-harm, promotes dangerous acts, or is otherwise illegal or harmful.

Protect Minors

Do not sexualize minors, solicit minors, share child sexual abuse material, groom users, or attempt to move minors into unsafe conversations. We report confirmed child sexual exploitation material to appropriate authorities where required.

Keep User-Generated Content Safe

These rules apply to usernames, avatars, custom topics, posts, comments, reactions, chat, clans, attachments, and reports. Do not evade filters, use coded abuse, or upload content that violates these Guidelines.

Fair Play

Do not cheat, exploit bugs, manipulate XP, use automation, impersonate other users, or interfere with matchmaking, scoring, or leaderboards.

Reporting And Blocking

Use in-app reporting tools to report users, posts, comments, messages, topics, or other content that violates these Guidelines. Use blocking tools to limit contact with another user. You can also contact info@kwiz.live.

Moderation

We may remove content, restrict features, reset scores, suspend accounts, ban users, preserve evidence, or escalate reports when needed to protect the community and comply with law.
