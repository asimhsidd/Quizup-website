How it plays

Three taps to a showdown.

Jump from the topic grid into a match, spend XP on clutch lifelines, and watch every round land with loud scoreboard feedback.

1

Pick a topic

Choose a popular topic, jump back into your last one, go random, or generate a quiz on whatever you can name.

2

Find a rival

Queue into live PvP, challenge a friend with a link, or send an async duel when they cannot play right now.

3

Win the round

Answer fast, spend XP on lifelines, protect streaks, and watch the scoreboard pop after every swing.

Built for the game

More than a quiz.

Live duels, custom topics, clans, a social feed, and rank progression, all in one fast game on your phone.
