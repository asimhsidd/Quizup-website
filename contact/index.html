Contact KWIZ

Use these contacts for account help, privacy requests, safety concerns, legal notices, and Google Play or OAuth verification review.

Email

General support: info@kwiz.live

Privacy requests: email info@kwiz.live with "Privacy request" in the subject.

Account deletion: email info@kwiz.live with "Account deletion request" in the subject if you cannot delete your account in the app.

Safety or abuse: email info@kwiz.live with "Safety report" in the subject.

Child safety: email info@kwiz.live with "Child safety" in the subject.

Helpful Details To Include

  • Your KWIZ username and account email, if relevant.
  • A link, screenshot, username, topic name, post, comment, message, or duel reference if you are reporting content.
  • The device, browser, or app version if you are reporting a technical issue.
  • Whether your request is urgent, privacy-related, child-safety-related, or account-related.
