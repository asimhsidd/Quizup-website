Child Safety Standards

KWIZ prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation and maintains reporting, review, and enforcement processes to help protect minors.

Our Standard

KWIZ has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation, grooming, sextortion, trafficking, sexual solicitation of minors, or any content that sexualizes or endangers children.

Prohibited Conduct

  • Uploading, sharing, requesting, threatening to share, or linking to child sexual abuse material.
  • Sexualized comments, roleplay, images, messages, or custom topics involving minors.
  • Grooming, coercion, sextortion, or attempts to move minors to unsafe channels.
  • Impersonating minors or adults to exploit or manipulate users.
  • Any attempt to evade detection, moderation, blocking, or reporting tools.

Reporting

Users can report accounts, posts, comments, messages, topics, or other content in the app. Urgent child safety concerns can also be emailed to info@kwiz.live with "Child safety" in the subject line.

Review And Enforcement

We review reports and may remove content, restrict features, suspend or ban accounts, preserve evidence, and cooperate with lawful investigations. Where required, confirmed child sexual abuse material may be reported to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children or the appropriate regional authority.

Prevention Measures

  • Community rules that prohibit exploitation, grooming, sexual content involving minors, harassment, and illegal content.
  • In-app reporting and blocking tools.
  • Moderation review for reports and abuse signals.
  • Filtering and safety checks for user-generated topics and content where available.
  • Account and content enforcement for violations.

Age

KWIZ is for people aged 16 and over. If you believe someone under 16 is using KWIZ or has provided personal information, contact us at info@kwiz.live.
